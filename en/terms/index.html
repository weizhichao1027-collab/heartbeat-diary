Pulse Plus

Pulse Plus offers monthly and annual auto-renewing subscriptions. Both provide 600 AI chat turns and 60 diary generations or chat summaries per month. Allowances reset by UTC calendar month, do not roll over, and are not granted for an entire year upfront.

Payment is charged through your Apple Account after confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically. You can cancel in system subscription settings; access continues through the current paid period. The purchase confirmation controls the final price and billing period.

Existing diaries, chats and generated content remain available to view and export after cancellation or expiry. Core mood recording, diary writing, running, weekly reviews, search, favorites, app lock and local backups do not require a subscription. Free includes 30 chat turns and 3 generations per month, plus 20 turns and 2 generations for first use on this device.

A chat turn consists of one sent message and one valid reply. Diary drafts and summaries share a generation allowance; regenerating uses another generation. Failed requests are not charged, and automatic retries are not charged twice.

Reference prices are CNY 18 per month or CNY 128 per year in mainland China, and $2.99 per month or $24.99 per year in the United States. Region, tax and final charges are shown in Apple's confirmation. Uninstalling does not cancel a subscription. Use the original Apple Account to restore purchases. Apple handles refunds. See subscription support.
